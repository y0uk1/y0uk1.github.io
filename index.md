## About Me

I am a Data Visualization Ph.D. student at Arizona State University working with [Chris Bryan](https://chrisbryan.github.io/).

I am interested in work at the intersection of GenAI, information visualization, and human-computer interaction.

Please refer to my [CV](./files/Yuki_Ueno_CV.pdf) for more information.

- **2025–present:** Ph.D. in Computer Science, Arizona State University
- **2019:** M.E. in Electrical Engineering, Kyoto University
- **2017:** B.E. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Kyoto University

## Research Interests

- **Data Visualization:** information visualization, visual cognition, visualization evaluation
- **Human–Computer Interaction:** AI-assisted interfaces, interactive algorithm visualization
